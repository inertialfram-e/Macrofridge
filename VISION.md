# MacroFridge: Vision & Product Strategy

## The Core Problem
Most households lose up to 30% of their purchased groceries to spoilage. Simultaneously, tracking nutritional goals (macros) remains a friction-heavy, manual process. Current solutions are either pure inventory trackers (lacking nutritional context) or pure macro trackers (lacking inventory context).

## The Mission
**MacroFridge** bridges the gap between the grocery receipt and the daily macro goal. We aim to eliminate food waste through "Use Soon" intelligence and automate nutritional optimization by generating recipes based on what's actually in the fridge.

## Target Audience
- **The Performance-Minded Household:** Athletes and fitness enthusiasts who need to hit specific macro targets but share a kitchen with others.
- **The Conscious Consumer:** Individuals looking to reduce their carbon footprint by minimizing food waste.
- **The Budget Optimizer:** Users who want to track grocery spending and maximize the "unit-price efficiency" of their pantry.

## Long-Term Goals (The "North Star")
1. **Zero-Friction Ingestion:** Snap a photo of a receipt, and your entire inventory is updated with macros and expiration dates in seconds.
2. **Heuristic Kitchen Assistant:** An AI that doesn't just suggest recipes, but proactively tells you: *"You have 3 chicken breasts and spinach expiring in 48 hours. If you make this curry, you'll hit your protein goal and save $12 in potential waste."*
3. **Hyper-Local Integration:** Seamlessly syncing with Apple Health and Google Fit to adjust recipe recommendations based on real-time activity levels.
